Privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Productivity Tracker (kristoffergt.com) is a small study and productivity app run by Kristoffer as a private individual. This page says what the app stores about you, who can see it, who helps run it, and how to get it deleted.

What is stored

  • Your account: email address, display name, your password (stored only as a one-way hash by the sign-in provider, never readable), your two-factor authentication setup if you turn it on, and your settings and preferences. If you sign in with Google, Kakao, GitHub or LinkedIn instead, the name, email address and profile picture link that service sends are stored with your account, and no password is kept unless you set one.
  • What you add: study logs, books and articles, job applications and the CVs and cover letters you upload, certifications, courses and syllabi, notes, writing samples, grammar notes, calendar events and reminders, trip expenses, messages and attachments you send to people you are linked with, and quiz results.
  • Phone notifications: if you switch on push notifications, the address your browser gives for sending them to that device.
  • Security: the sign-in provider keeps logs of sign-ins. The app keeps the IP address of wrong site-PIN guesses for up to one day, only to limit guessing.
  • On your device: your sign-in session and a few display preferences are kept in your browser's local storage.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Videos embedded in notes use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) player.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • People you link your account with, for the categories you choose to share in your sharing settings.
  • Everyone signed in sees your display name and totals on the leaderboards, unless you turn on "hide me from leaderboards".
  • If you share a file's short link, anyone you give the link to can open that file.
  • The administrator (Kristoffer) can see the list of accounts and manage them, for example to confirm an email address, reset a password or delete an account.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for marketing.

Services that help run the app

How long it is kept

Until you delete it, or until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Account settings → Delete account) removes your account and everything you added, and the files you uploaded are removed within a day.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or delete anything you added, from the app.
  • Turn emails and notifications off in Notification settings, or with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for anything to be corrected or deleted, by writing to the contact address below.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), stored data is encrypted at rest, uploaded CVs and cover letters are private and opened through short-lived links, and you can protect your account with two-factor authentication.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
